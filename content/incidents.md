Every homelab blog shows the finished thing. This page is the other half: what broke, how I
actually noticed, and what it taught me. Some of these are still open — an entry that sits at
**open** for months is more useful than one quietly flipped to fixed.

The column that matters most is **how it was found**. "I happened to look" appears more than I'd
like, and that's the argument for better detection rather than better intentions.
