## In Plain Terms

This blog is just a personal site I created, with no intent of selling, advertising, or taking your information. It's my own private blog that I share for career purposes.

## What That Means

- No accounts, no forms, and nothing here asks for your details.
- No advertising, and no tracking for commercial purposes.
- Nothing is sold or shared with anyone.
- The site exists to document my own technical projects and work.

## What Does Run

One third-party service is on the page, and it's fair to say so rather than claim nothing is.

- **Page counts (GoatCounter).** A cookie-free counter so I can see which posts get read. It records that a page was viewed and where the visit came from; it sets no cookie and keeps no personal identifier.

There is no comment system. If you want to say something about a post, the links on the [home page](/) are the way.

The site itself is static files served by Cloudflare Pages, which handles the requests the way any host does. There's no server-side code of mine and no database.

## Contact

If you have any questions about this site, you can reach me through the links on the [home page](/).
